# Presentation

How the site looks and presents itself: icons, the logo, the favicon, the social card, and the style of its text. Epic: #56.

## Icons and logo

- **Replace Lucide with Phosphor** (product manager, 2026-11-10), for Phosphor's MIT license. Built in #55.
- **The logo, `site/favicon.svg`, and `site/social-card.png` move to Phosphor too** (product manager, 2026-11-10). The social card is generated by code from the logo.
- **Phosphor's Bold weight** (product manager, 2026-11-10), closer to the old icons' line weight than Regular.
- **Phosphor is credited in one line on the Documentation page,** "Icons by Phosphor Icons, MIT License.", under "Credits and Attribution" (product manager, 2026-11-10). The license ships as `site/licenses/phosphor.txt`, following the usual MIT practice. Built in #55.

## Text style

- **Everything written follows the Google developer style guide, with capitalization from the Microsoft Writing Style Guide** (sentence case) (product manager, 2026-11-11).
- **The existing text is brought to that rule** (product manager, 2026-11-11), on every page, in the Changelog's past entries, and in the README. Tab titles keep the " - " separator. Planned in #65, which starts before the Convert stories (#62, #63, #64).
